openOODA.org

Autonomous Agentic Architecture & OODA Loop Systems

openOODA is a capability-secure systems language and deterministic runtime designed specifically for the age of sovereign AI agents. Processes are born with zero ambient authority: every effect—from network socket allocation to filesystem modification—requires an explicit, unforgeable capability token (&NetCap, &TcpCap, &FsCap). Featuring an autonomous Observe → Orient → Decide → Act hardware loop, openOODA guarantees empirical replayability, IEEE 754 precision, and mathematically verifiable effect boundaries.
